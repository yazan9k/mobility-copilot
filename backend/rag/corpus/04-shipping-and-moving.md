# Shipping and Moving Allowance

*This document describes policy for Meridian Systems, a fictional company. All figures and volumes are illustrative.*

## Overview

Meridian arranges and pays for the shipment of household goods for employees whose assignment type qualifies. Shipping is booked through Meridian's appointed moving partner; employees do not arrange their own move and claim it back. Where an employee wishes to use a different mover, they may do so at their own cost and forgo the entitlement, but the company will not reimburse against a third-party invoice.

## Eligibility

Household shipping is available to employees on a **permanent transfer** and to employees on a **long-term assignment** of six to twenty-four months.

Employees on a **short-term assignment** of under six months receive no household shipping entitlement. This follows from the nature of a short-term assignment: the employee retains their home residence, lives in temporary or per-diem-funded accommodation in the destination as described in `03-housing-stipend.md`, and is not establishing a household. Short-term assignees are instead entitled to an excess baggage allowance of two additional checked bags on the outbound and return flights, booked through the company travel provider.

Short-term assignees occasionally ask whether they can ship a small consignment in place of the baggage allowance, or whether a partial shipping entitlement exists at their band. Neither is available. The entitlement is determined by assignment type alone, and band does not create a shipping entitlement where the assignment type does not.

## Allowance by household size

Where an employee is eligible, the shipping volume is determined by household size rather than by band. A director relocating alone receives the same volume entitlement as an early-career employee relocating alone.

| Relocating household | Sea freight volume | Air freight volume |
|---|---|---|
| Employee only | 20 cubic metres | 100 kg |
| Employee + 1 dependent | 30 cubic metres | 150 kg |
| Employee + 2 or more dependents | 40 cubic metres | 250 kg |

Both entitlements may be used on the same move. The air freight allowance exists so that an employee can send ahead the items they need in the first weeks — clothing, work equipment, children's essentials — while the sea consignment is in transit.

## Transit times

Sea freight typically takes six to ten weeks door to door depending on the corridor, plus destination customs clearance, which is fastest in Tier 1 and slowest in Tier 3. Air freight typically takes one to two weeks. Employees should plan on the assumption that sea freight will not arrive before the temporary accommodation period ends, which is why the air allowance matters more than its size suggests.

Customs clearance in the destination generally requires the employee to hold a residence permit or equivalent registration. A shipment can therefore be delayed by a permit delay even where the freight itself has arrived. This dependency is set out in `06-relocation-timeline.md`.

## Excluded items

The following are not shipped at company cost under any assignment type: motor vehicles, boats and trailers; hazardous materials including paint, solvents, and pressurised containers; perishable goods; plants; firearms and ammunition; and items whose import is restricted in the destination.

Pets are not shipped as freight. Pet relocation is an eligible use of the relocation allowance under `01-relocation-allowance.md` and is arranged by the employee.

Employees intending to ship a vehicle should be aware that several destinations impose import duties exceeding the vehicle's value. Meridian does not advise on vehicle import and does not meet the cost.

## Storage

Where a permanent transferee's sea consignment arrives before they have secured a lease, Meridian meets destination storage costs for up to sixty days. Storage beyond sixty days is at the employee's cost.

Origin storage — retaining goods in the home country rather than shipping them — is available to long-term assignees for the duration of the assignment, up to 15 cubic metres. It is not available to permanent transferees, who are expected to ship or dispose of their household, and it is not available to short-term assignees, who have no shipping entitlement to substitute.

## Insurance

Meridian insures the shipment at replacement value through the moving partner, subject to a declared inventory. Employees must complete the inventory and declare high-value items above 2,000 EUR equivalent individually. Items not declared are covered only at a standard per-kilogram rate, which will be substantially below replacement value for anything valuable.

Claims are made through the moving partner within thirty days of delivery. Meridian does not process shipping claims directly and cannot adjudicate a disputed claim; where a claim is refused and the employee disputes the outcome, the matter should be raised with a Global Mobility adviser.

## Return shipping

Return shipping at the end of an assignment is covered on the same volume basis as the outbound move. Entitlement and process are set out in `10-repatriation-and-assignment-end.md`, including the circumstances in which return shipping is forfeited.

## Exceptions

Requests to exceed the volume entitlement, to ship an excluded item, or to extend storage beyond the covered period are discretionary. They cannot be approved from this policy and must be escalated to a Global Mobility adviser under `08-escalation-criteria.md`.
